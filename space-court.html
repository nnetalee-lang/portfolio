Space Court

Tennis partner matching & performance analytics platform

Space Court is a mobile app designed to solve a problem tennis players keep running into: finding compatible playing partners. It combines skill-based matchmaking with location services, performance tracking and access to professional coaching, in one place.

Project overview

Role
Product Designer, student project
Timeline
3 months
Platform
iOS

01 / Executive summary

The challenge and the solution

  • The problem

    Tennis players consistently struggle to find partners that match their skill level, location and availability. Existing options are spread across social media groups, club message boards and informal networks, which creates friction and missed games.

    • No central place to find tennis partners
    • Hard to match players by skill level
    • Little visibility into nearby courts and availability
    • No objective performance tracking or improvement metrics

  • The solution

    Space Court is a matchmaking platform that connects players by skill level, distance and schedule. It adds performance analytics, court discovery and access to professional coaching.

    • Smart matchmaking: partner matching by skill and location
    • Performance tracking: statistics and progress over time
    • Court discovery: nearby courts with availability and ratings
    • Pro coaching: connect with certified trainers for lessons

02 / The matchmaking logic

Finding the perfect match

The core matchmaking system looks at several factors to connect players: skill ratings, distance, schedule availability and playing style.

Compatibility algorithm

A proposed weighting for this student project. It was not validated with users.

  • 35%

    Skill rating

    NTRP-based skill matching within ±0.5

  • 30%

    Location

    Distance with a customizable radius (1–25 miles)

  • 20%

    Availability

    Overlap in preferred playing times

  • 15%

    Play style

    Competitive level, game format and surface preference
