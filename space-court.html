Space Court

Tennis Partner App

Tennis partner matching and performance analytics

Space Court is a mobile app for a problem tennis players keep running into: finding compatible playing partners. It combines skill-based matchmaking with location, performance tracking and access to coaches, all in one place.

Executive summary

The challenge and the solution

  • The problem

    Tennis players struggle to find partners who match their skill level, location and availability. Existing options are spread across social media groups, club message boards and informal networks, which creates friction and missed games.

    • No central place to find tennis partners
    • Hard to match players by skill level
    • Little visibility into nearby courts and availability
    • No objective performance tracking or improvement metrics

  • The solution

    Space Court connects players by skill level, distance and schedule. It adds performance analytics, court discovery and access to coaches.

    Smart matchmaking
    Partner matching by skill and location
    Performance tracking
    Statistics and progress over time
    Court discovery
    Nearby courts with availability and ratings
    Coaching
    Connect with certified trainers for lessons

Insights

User research

To understand what players need from a partner-finding app, I ran a survey with 18 tennis players (16 men and 2 women) about their challenges and preferences.

  • 85%

    struggle to find games with suitable partners, because of coordinating schedules and finding available courts.

  • 75%

    are looking for tennis partners but find it hard to find suitable matches.

  • 45%

    are concerned about small player pools and mismatched skill levels.

The matchmaking logic

Finding the right partner

Matchmaking looks at several factors to connect players: skill rating, distance, schedule availability and playing style.

Compatibility algorithm (design hypothesis)

A proposed weighting for this student project. It was not validated with users.

    Availability

    Overlap in preferred playing times

  • 15%

    Play style

Performance analytics

Seeing how you improve

An analytics dashboard that tracks game statistics, performance trends and skill progression, so players can see how they improve over time.
