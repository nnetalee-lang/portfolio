Development Intelligence Platform

B2B analytics for R&D leaders

An early-stage startup building a development-intelligence platform. It unifies data from Git, project management and GenAI tools to show R&D leaders how their teams build software, and what it costs.

I was the only designer, working with three engineers and early customers. I defined and designed new features based on what customers needed to see, and redesigned a product that until then had been designed by the engineers themselves.

The challenge

Critical data is scattered across tools, with no single source of truth

Leaders can’t clearly understand productivity, AI impact, or how resources map to business goals.

Pain points

  • Data is fragmented across multiple tools with no unified view.
  • There is no clear way to measure the impact of GenAI on productivity or quality.
  • Engineering effort is not translated into cost, ROI or business value.
  • Leaders are expected to make decisions without reliable, consistent data.

The solution

One analytics layer for all the data, with a view for each stakeholder

It delivers GenAI ROI, productivity, quality and cost insights to support real decision-making.

Key features

  • Unifies data from Git, project tools, GenAI and HR into a single analytics layer.
  • Quantifies the impact of GenAI on productivity, quality and development speed.
  • Combines code and process metrics to reveal trends in performance and quality.
  • Translates engineering activity into time, cost and ROI insights tied to business goals.

Who it’s for

Two leaders, two different questions

Both look at the same data, but at a different altitude. That’s why the platform opens on an organization-wide view and lets each user drill down to a team or a single developer.

  • VP R&D

    Runs a large R&D organization across several regions, and answers to the C-level.

    “Is our engineering budget going to the right places?”

    Needs to

    • Manage budget and operational efficiency
    • Report team performance to the C-level
    • Spot risks, delays and inefficiencies early

    Answered by: Jira Spend, GenAI impact in Activity

  • Engineering manager

    Leads a team of 12–15 engineers, and needs to show its impact to leadership.

    “Where is my team stuck, and how do I prove what we deliver?”

    Needs to

    • Track individual and team productivity
    • Find bottlenecks in the development workflow
    • Justify resource allocation decisions

    Answered by: Activity, Developer profile

Research and insights

Competitive analysis

Out of five competitors I analyzed in depth, I’m highlighting three here, with their main features, strengths and limitations. It’s a snapshot of the full research that guided my design decisions.

  • Competitor 1

    Key features
    Cycle time metrics, Automation bot, Slack integration
    Strength
    Intuitive, easy-to-use interface
    Limitation
    No cost or ROI insights

    Lacks financial reporting

  • Competitor 2

    Key features
    Code-level reporting, PR and commit analytics, Benchmarking
    Strength
    Detailed insights into code
    Limitation
    Limited team performance breakdowns

    Less focused on team leaders

  • Competitor 3

    Key features
    Engineering dashboards, Correlates DevOps data, Review workflow metrics
    Strength
    Comprehensive set of reports
    Limitation
    No deep dive into GenAI impact

    Focuses on DevOps practices

  • Insight 01

    Most tools don’t talk money

    Added financial indicators to every chart

    • Quarterly savings comparisons
    • Tooltips translating % to $
    • Direct links to Jira cost items
  • Insight 02

    There’s data, but no depth

    Built drill-down into every widget

    • Navigation from group → team → individual
    • Filtering by repo, quarter or GenAI tool
    • Quick access to team-specific insights
  • Insight 03

    GenAI is present but not measured

    Designed a dedicated GenAI impact dashboard

    • KPIs comparing AI vs. non-AI contributions
    • Time saved per pull request
    • Usage trends by group and quarter
